💡 Power-User Tip: Address Bar Search Shortcut (Brave / Chrome)

If you want to type b <scripture> (e.g. b John 3:16) directly in your browser's address bar to jump straight to Bible.com:

  1. Open browser settings to Site search (brave://settings/searchEngines or chrome://settings/searchEngines).
  2. Add a site search shortcut with:
    Shortcut: b
    URL: https://www.bible.com/search/bible?q=%s
  3. Deep Bible automatically intercepts scripture searches from that URL and instantly jumps straight to the reader in your active translation!
