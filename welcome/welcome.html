What Deep Bible Does For You

Deep Bible converts Bible.com into an ad-free, clutter-free academic study sanctuary. It hides gamified streak banners, promotional videos, and bulky sidebars, replacing them with a sleek floating study bar, instant 1-key translation switching (press E for ESV, N for NIV, S for NASB, B for BSB, K for NKJV), verse-by-verse commentaries (David Guzik, TGC, Constable, Calvin, Matthew Henry), Strong's Greek & Hebrew Lexicon, Treasury of Scripture Knowledge cross-references, parallel translation comparison, and immersive themes—including an authentic Ancient Illuminated Scroll!
