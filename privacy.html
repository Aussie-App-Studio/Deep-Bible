Privacy Policy for Deep Bible

Last Updated: November 3, 2026 Developer: Aussie App Studio GitHub Repository ↗

Privacy-by-Design Architecture

  • No Personal Data Collected: Deep Bible never collects, stores, tracks, or sells personal information.
  • No External Trackers: No analytics libraries, marketing beacons, or tracking pixels are embedded.
  • Local Browser Storage: All your custom reading preferences, theme choices, and hotkeys stay safely inside your own browser.
  • Transparent Open Source: The complete source code is publicly audited and available on GitHub.

1. What Data Deep Bible Interacts With

All user preferences and settings operate entirely client-side on your device:

Data Type Purpose Storage Location
Reader Preferences Saves active reading themes (Sepia, Dark, OLED, Historical), font family, font size, and reading column width. Local Browser Storage (chrome.storage.sync)
Translation Shortcuts Saves single-key version mappings (e.g. E for ESV, N for NIV) and added custom translations. Local Browser Storage (chrome.storage.sync)
Distraction-Free Toggles Saves toggles for hiding header bars, sidebars, and banners. Local Browser Storage (chrome.storage.sync)
Parallel Compare Stores translations selected for side-by-side or interleaved study. Local Browser Storage (chrome.storage.sync)

2. Chrome Extension Permissions Declared

Deep Bible complies strictly with Google Chrome Web Store's Minimum Permissions Policy:

  • storage: Persists user-selected preferences, hotkeys, and themes locally in the browser.
  • https://www.bible.com/*: Injects the study toolbar, 1-key translation swapper, and distraction-free reader into Bible.com.
  • https://moments.youversionapi.com/*: Used strictly to resolve official Bible.com version abbreviations when adding custom translations, and to sync user-initiated highlights with YouVersion's API. No user credentials or session tokens are stored by Deep Bible.
  • https://biblehub.com/*: Fetches Strong's Greek & Hebrew lexicon entries and TSK cross-references on user demand.
  • https://*.enduringword.com/*, https://*.soniclight.com/*, https://*.freebiblecommentary.org/*, https://*.thegospelcoalition.org/*: Fetches verse commentaries on user demand when the study drawer is opened.

Content Fetching Privacy: Requests to commentary and lexicon providers query only the biblical chapter/verse passage (e.g. ROM.8.1) to fetch study text. No user IDs, IP logs, or cookies are collected or retained by the developer.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data resides locally on your computer, you retain full control over your data:

  • You can reset all settings anytime by clicking "↺ Reset All Defaults" in the extension settings.
  • Uninstalling Deep Bible from chrome://extensions/ permanently wipes all local extension preferences.

4. Single-Purpose Policy

Deep Bible's single focused purpose is to enhance Scripture study on Bible.com by providing academic study tools (verse commentaries, Strong's lexicon, cross-references, translation switching, and distraction-free formatting).
